5. Adjunction rather than naïve inversion

The strongest mathematically conservative relation is initially an adjunction:

\mathfrak W_\Theta
\dashv
\mathfrak U_\Theta,

with unit

\eta:
\operatorname{Id}
\Rightarrow
\mathfrak U_\Theta\mathfrak W_\Theta

and counit

\varepsilon:
\mathfrak W_\Theta\mathfrak U_\Theta
\Rightarrow
\operatorname{Id}.

Why an adjunction?

Because weaving and unweaving generally live in different categories:

* weaving maps local data into relationally richer structures;
* unweaving returns a reconstruction, explanation or decomposition;
* neither direction must be strictly invertible;
* the unit and counit expose the exact return defect.

The triangle identities become the first closure tests.

When they fail only up to specified comparison cells, the proper object is a bicategorical or higher adjunction, and the failure itself is retained as higher holonomy.
