# CDCL topology-transfer experiment: pre-registered design

Date: 2026-10-29. Branch: `dev/kakeya-violation-frame`. Previous, superseding evidence: [signed-orbit report](VIOLATION_SIGNED_CONNECTION_ORBIT_REPORT.md). Status **H** until an external CDCL runner produces machine-readable **M**. The prior learned incidence and signed-connection predictors lost to classical structure in small DPLL tests; do not erase that conclusion.

**Research question.** On *independently generated and non-identical* CNFs larger than the 12-variable truth-table pilots, does signed clause-variable holonomy improve out-of-generator and out-of-size prediction of CDCL work, beyond unsigned topology, variable degree and polarity structure? The object is the finite signed incidence graph. This is neither the Kakeya maximal theorem nor a SAT complexity theorem.

**Frozen data/target.** Generators: uniform random 3-CNF, local ring 3-CNF, four-community 3-CNF, hub-biased 3-CNF, degree-three prism Tseitin, and reconnected degree-three Tseitin. Declared Boolean variables per formula 48, 72, 96; formula seeds independent and documented. The Tseitin generators use cubic graphs with 32, 48 or 64 vertices, respectively; charges are deliberately even/odd by seed to test SAT and UNSAT conditions without using solver labels for features. We shall also inspect non-isomorphism of distinct seeded cubic graph instances, not just their raw labeled edge lists.

Run PySAT Glucose4 (`g4`) as an *external experimental CDCL instrument*, with fixed per-call conflict budget (default 2,000), three signed relabelings (same formula, independent solver start per orientation) and no previously learnt clauses shared across runs. Record reported conflicts, decisions, propagations, result (SAT/UNSAT/UNKNOWN), per-call wall time and total feature time. SAT model, when present, must replay on that **input** CNF; UNSAT from the external solver is a benchmark observation, not a λSAT certified result. UNKNOWN is **right censored**, never mislabeled as easy or as a decision. The primary target is log2(1+min(reported conflicts, budget)) and the censoring fraction is reported beside every error. Do not treat this budgeted statistic as an uncensored latent true conflict count.

**Predictors and fair controls.** Frozen ridge regression penalty alpha=10 and train-only feature scaling. Baseline: declared n, m/n, clause width, variable occurrence CV/maximum, polarity imbalance, XOR-clause-pattern fraction, plus **unsigned** connected component count, cycle rank and four-cycle density. Experimental extras: signed negative 4-cycle fraction, unbalanced-component fraction and normalized negative-square count. Null: deterministic same-sign, degree- and polarity-margin-preserving support edge swaps. This null changes the formula semantics and graph topology, so is a **feature-null only**, not a SAT matching pair. Report successful switches; a zero-switch null is invalid and must not be counted as meaningful control. Solver outputs must never be used in feature extraction.

**Splits.** Leave-one-generator-family-out (LOFO) and train on smaller n, test on the largest n. No re-training after inspection of held-out errors. Include both SAT and UNSAT where possible, but do not claim a balanced outcome distribution unless measured. Report per-family errors, unique graph topology checks, missing/UNKNOWN counts and feature-to-CDCL work overhead.

**Primary prediction.** At least 0.03 reduction in mean absolute **log2-capped-conflicts** error over the full LOFO sample against baseline, and outperform the nontrivially mixing support-swapped null, with >=80% completed calls. Secondary prediction: incremental gain on the held-out largest n and extraction cost less than 10% of solver wall time at median. These are conservative *promotion gates*, not something CI should assert as a mandatory success.

**Kill conditions.** If the signed features lose to the baseline or to the matched null, retire this specific prediction in the tested regime. If too many calls are censored, or null switches systematically fail, report **inconclusive**, not success or null equivalence. If the solver label, feature extraction or isomorphism gate leaks across splits, invalidate that run. If geometric features cost more than solving, no production routing promotion even if predictive accuracy improves.

**Provenance and scope.** Canary contributes cheap degree statistics and family-stratified ablation, not inferred `A4` or `Z2×Z2` group identities; OpenAI ETH and Kakeya papers motivate questions but imply none of these CDCL predictions. No certified solver path, clause preprocessing, or `main` is changed. All evidence remains finite **M**, and algorithmic/general asymptotic claims remain **H**.

Reproduction contract: small deterministic CI smoke suite plus raw JSON artifacts, with explicit versions for Python/PySAT and exact family, seed, n, solver, budgets, outcome coverage and feature time. The CI outcome is *validity* rather than a guaranteed geometry win.
