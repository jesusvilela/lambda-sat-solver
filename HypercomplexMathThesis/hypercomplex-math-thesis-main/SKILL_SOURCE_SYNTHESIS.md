## 5. Fano/octonion and braid proof layer

The UTAI/Bunny and braid-prover skills require the thesis to track nonassociativity explicitly.

Let `\mathbb O` be the octonions with Fano multiplication. The associator is

```math
[a,b,c]=(ab)c-a(bc).
```

The anti-associator or nonassociative correction operator is a computational primitive, not decorative language:

```math
\S OCT\_CORRECT(a,b,c)=\operatorname{control}([a,b,c]).
```

For braid ensembles, a state can carry a braid word

```math
B_n=\sigma_{i_1}^{\epsilon_1}\cdots\sigma_{i_k}^{\epsilon_k},
\quad \epsilon_j\in\{-1,+1\}.
```

The proof layer asks whether kernel dimensions, Berry phases, and braid transformations persist under covers.
