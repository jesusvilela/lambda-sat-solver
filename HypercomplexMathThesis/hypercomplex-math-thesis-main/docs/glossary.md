# Glossary

## Hypercomplex learning tower

A four-floor method for solving by immersion, geometry, hypercomplex lift, and grounded return.

## Immersion

The act of entering a problem's geometry rather than observing it only from outside.

## Resonance

Constraint-coupling between solver and problem, or between two states, without total collapse of distinction.

## Absorption

Failure mode where resonance destroys returnability or identity distinction.

## Hypercomplex lift

Embedding a problem into a richer reservoir where real, complex, quaternionic, octonionic, symbolic, narrative, empirical, or categorical representations can coexist.

## Adiabatic breathing

Slow modulation of manifold parameters such that the internal system can adapt without destabilization.

## Grounding operator

A projection from transformed high-dimensional state to usable result:

```math
\S GROUND : \widetilde S \to S_{\mathrm{usable}}
```

## TELOS

A directional substrate field, modeled initially as:

```math
\S TELOS(X)=\nabla_g\Phi(X)
```

where `\Phi` balances coherence, resonance, recognition, absorption risk, drift, and returnability.

## Mind invariant

A quantity or relation preserved across immersion, lift, rotation, and return.

## Gödelian fold

A self-reference event where a system's description loops into the system being described.

## Bunny/UTAI formalization culture

A style of development that separates claim, metaphor, executable evidence, Lean theorem, and frontier gap honestly.
