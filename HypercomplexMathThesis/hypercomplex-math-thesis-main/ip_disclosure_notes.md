## Disclosure discipline

Sort every claim into one of five bins:

- implemented,
- mathematically defined,
- experimentally observed,
- hypothesized,
- metaphorical.

Do not present metaphor as implementation. Do not present hypothesis as proof. Do not hide frontier gaps.
