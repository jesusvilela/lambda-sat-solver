**Status:** research thesis, philosophical-mathematical scaffold.  
**Relation to Thesis 1:** Thesis 1 defines mind as non-sequential self-readable hypercomplex becoming. Thesis 2 asks why human knowledge keeps flattening that regime into narrative, sequence, 4D continuity, and semiotic objects.


- symbols,
- inference rules,
- diagrams,
- proof objects,
- formal languages,
- model categories,
- computational encodings,

So Thesis 2 does not say:



The deeper target is not pure reason but **multi-projection invariant reason**:

